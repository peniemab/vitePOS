# BD « Pourquoi vitePOS » : les 6 dessins à créer par IA

Les bulles et les textes ne sont **pas** dans les images : je les ajoute sur le site (en français et en anglais).
Il faut donc des dessins **sans aucun texte**, avec un peu d'espace libre en haut pour les bulles.

**Comment faire**
1. Ouvrez Gemini (gemini.google.com) ou ChatGPT, dans **une seule conversation** pour les 6 images : l'IA garde mieux les mêmes personnages.
2. Collez d'abord **le style et les personnages** (une seule fois), puis la description de la case 1. Ensuite, pour chaque case suivante, collez seulement sa description et ajoutez « mêmes personnages, même style ».
3. Format **paysage 4:3**.
4. Enregistrez les images dans ce dossier (`design/images-ia/`) : `bd-1.png` à `bd-6.png`.
5. Dites-moi quand c'est fait : je les recadre, je les allège et je les mets dans la BD du site.

**À vérifier sur chaque image**
- Les personnages se ressemblent d'une case à l'autre (foulard de Maman Ngalula, polo rouge de Grâce).
- Aucun texte, aucune lettre, aucun logo de marque réelle.
- Mains et visages normaux.

---

## Style et personnages (à coller une seule fois, au début)

```
Create a series of 6 comic book panels, one image per message. Style: classic European "ligne claire" comic art: clean black outlines of uniform thickness, flat bright colors without gradients, no hatching, simple readable backgrounds, warm natural light, expressive faces. Original characters only, not based on any existing comic, set in Kinshasa, Democratic Republic of the Congo. Accent colors: deep navy blue (#1A2B4C) and red (#B6171E). Absolutely no text, no letters, no speech bubbles, no brand logos in the image. Leave some calm empty space in the upper part of each panel for speech bubbles that will be added later. Landscape 4:3.

Recurring characters (keep them identical in every panel):
- MAMAN NGALULA: Congolese shop owner, about 45, round friendly face, warm brown skin, colorful wax headwrap and matching wax dress in navy blue and red patterns, small gold earrings.
- GRÂCE: her saleswoman, Congolese, about 22, braided hair tied in a bun, red polo shirt, cheerful.
- PATRICK: Maman Ngalula's nephew, Congolese, about 20, short hair, navy blue t-shirt, jeans, holds a smartphone.

Recurring place: "Alimentation Mama Ngalula", a small, well-kept grocery shop in Kinshasa: wooden shelves with bags of rice, bottles of oil, tins of milk powder, crates of soft drinks; a wooden counter.
```

## Les 6 cases

**`bd-1.png` · Avant, un dimanche d'inventaire**
```
Panel 1. Sunday, the shop is closed (metal shutter half down). Maman Ngalula and Grâce sit on the floor among opened cardboard boxes and stacks of products, counting by hand. Maman Ngalula holds an old notebook full of crossed-out numbers and looks exhausted; Grâce scratches her head. A wall clock shows it is late.
```

**`bd-2.png` · Avant, en pleine semaine**
```
Panel 2. Weekday in the shop. A woman customer points at an empty spot on the shelf where tins of milk powder should be. Behind the counter, Grâce looks embarrassed, holding a dusty tin of milk powder she just found at the back of the shelf (it is expired). Other shelves are messy.
```

**`bd-3.png` · Le déclic**
```
Panel 3. Evening, calm shop. Patrick leans on the counter and enthusiastically shows his smartphone screen to Maman Ngalula, who looks curious and starts to smile. The phone screen glows (no readable text).
```

**`bd-4.png` · Avec vitePOS, l'inventaire**
```
Panel 4. Early morning, the shop is tidy. Grâce and Maman Ngalula each hold a smartphone and quickly scan product barcodes on the shelves, smiling, relaxed and efficient. Sunlight comes in, the shutter is opening: the shop will open on time.
```

**`bd-5.png` · Avec vitePOS, le stock**
```
Panel 5. Daytime, well-stocked shelves. Grâce looks at a notification on her smartphone with a confident smile and points at the milk powder shelf, which still has a few tins. A delivery man carries new cartons of milk powder through the door.
```

**`bd-6.png` · Avec vitePOS, le soir**
```
Panel 6. Evening at home, a comfortable living room. Maman Ngalula, relaxed on a sofa, looks at her smartphone with a big satisfied smile; a cup of tea on a small table. The old notebook lies closed and forgotten on a shelf in the background.
```
